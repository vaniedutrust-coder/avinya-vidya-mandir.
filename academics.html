Curriculum in Action

The 3-Pillar Learning Framework

Constructed upon the National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS). Here is exactly what children experience, how they learn, and what they develop at each stage.

1

Playgroup & Pre-Nursery

The Gentle Beginning: Joy, Safety & Sensory Wonder
Ages 2 to 3 Years
📖 What Children Learn
  • Verbal self-expression and basic daily needs communication
  • Rhythm, nursery melodies, and bilingual nursery rhymes
  • Visual recognition of primary colors and tactile textures
  • Body awareness, gentle manners, and mealtime routines
🧩 How They Learn
  • Guided sensory stations with soft sand, water, and dough
  • Interactive picture-book storytelling and puppet drama
  • Gross-motor crawling tunnels, mini-slides, and obstacle mats
  • Our signature "Happy Child Settling" gentle transition care
🌱 What They Develop
  • Emotional comfort separating from parents with smiling trust
  • Fine-motor pincer grip and hand-eye coordination
  • Peer empathy, turn-taking, and active listening habits
  • Natural curiosity and excitement to come to school every day
2

Nursery & Lower Kindergarten (LKG)

Curiosity & Expression: Phonics, Numbers & Discovery
Ages 3 to 5 Years
📖 What Children Learn
  • Synthetic phonics: 42 letter sounds and basic sound blending
  • Concrete numeracy: Counting 1–20, sorting, and pattern recognition
  • Environmental inquiry: Flora, fauna, seasons, and family community
  • Pre-writing stroke formation and expressive drawing
🧩 How They Learn
  • Montessori sensorial manipulatives, beads, and sound cards
  • Daily morning circle conversation and show-and-tell
  • Campus nature discovery walks across our 1-acre courtyard
  • Water play, paint exploration, and structured pretend-play corners
🌱 What They Develop
  • Early phonemic awareness and confidence in reading simple CVC words
  • Logical classification and spatial problem-solving
  • Bilingual vocabulary confidence in English and Hindi
  • Collaborative play habits and respectful classroom conduct
3

Upper Kindergarten (UKG) & Class 1

Conceptual Grounding: Reading Fluency, Math & Inquiry
Ages 5 to 7 Years
📖 What Children Learn
  • Independent sentence reading, creative writing, and comprehension
  • Foundational arithmetic: Addition, subtraction, shapes & measurement
  • Environmental Studies (EVS): Habitats, plants, water & civic values
  • Computational thinking fundamentals and bilingual presentation
🧩 How They Learn
  • Concrete-to-abstract manipulatives (number lines, base-10 blocks)
  • Hands-on science experiments and botanical seed germination
  • Guided writing journals, dramatized reading circles, and poetry
  • Outdoor sports turf athletics, agility circuits, and team relays
🌱 What They Develop
  • Fluency in reading storybooks and writing personal thoughts
  • Mathematical intuition and quantitative reasoning
  • Independent study habits, sustained focus, and self-discipline
  • Complete school readiness for seamless progression into primary grades
Our Teaching Method

How Learning Happens Every Day

We blend the best of early-childhood research into four tangible, daily classroom practices that ensure no child is left behind.

🔤

Synthetic Phonics & Literacy

Children learn letters through sounds and multisensory gestures, rapidly acquiring reading fluency without stressful memorization.

🔢

Concrete-First Mathematics

Math is experienced hands-on with wooden rods, counting beads, and tactile geometry before ever putting pencil to worksheet numbers.

🌿

1-Acre Nature Discovery

Our expansive outdoor courtyard and sports turf serve as open-air classrooms for plant care, insect observation, and weather inquiry.

❤️

1:10 Nurturing Mentorship

With small class cohorts and an attentive educator ratio, every child's voice is heard, respected, and gently guided every single day.

📊

Zero-Stress Formative Observation

We do not subject young toddlers to high-stakes written examinations. Instead, teachers observe children during daily activities, recording milestones across vocabulary, numeracy, motor agility, and social cooperation in comprehensive developmental portfolios.

🤝

Active Parent Partnerships

Parents receive regular progress updates, developmental portfolio showcases, and dedicated one-on-one educator dialogues. If a child needs extra encouragement in phonics or motor skills, educators partner with parents immediately to provide personalized support.
