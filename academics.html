Intellectual Rigor & Play-Way Mastery

The Avinya Academic Journey: Playgroup to K-12

From the gentle wonder of Playgroup to the conceptual rigor of Class 1 and our planned annual expansion toward CBSE Senior Secondary graduation, our curriculum creates fearless, joyful lifelong learners.

Active Today on Campus

Foundational Stage Programs (Ages 2 to 7)

Constructed upon the National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS) and Montessori play-way methodology.

Age 2–3 Years
Playgroup & Pre-Nursery
The Gentle Beginning

Social integration, motor coordination, sensory stimulation, language immersion through nursery rhymes, sandbox play, and our signature "Happy Child Settling" care.

✓ Zero Pressure · 1:10 Caretaker Ratio
Age 3–5 Years
Nursery & LKG
Curiosity & Expression

Synthetic phonics, pre-writing pencil grip exercises, color sorting, number recognition, dramatization, water play, and nature exploration in our courtyard garden.

✓ Storytelling & Sensory Science
Age 5–7 Years
UKG & Class 1
Conceptual Grounding

Formal introduction to reading fluency, bilingual speech (Hindi & English), foundational arithmetic, environmental studies (EVS), coding logic, and formal physical education.

✓ CBSE Foundational Benchmarks
Strategic Roadmap

Our Annual Expansion: Growing to CBSE K-12

Our 1-acre campus infrastructure affords the physical space, laboratory allocations, and athletic facilities to support our children as they grow, adding one class annually until Class 12.

Active Now Playgroup – Class 1

Foundational Stage & Class 1 Cohort

Currently nurturing students through early childhood up to Class 1. Small cohorts ensure dedicated attention, habit formation, and foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN).

Phase 1 Growth Class 2 to Class 5

Preparatory & Primary Expansion

Each academic year, a new class is inaugurated for our advancing batch. Expansion includes dedicated junior science discovery labs, computer education, and inter-school sports leagues.

Phase 2 Growth Class 6 to Class 8

Middle School Exploratory Stage

Specialized faculty across Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Advanced Mathematics, Social Sciences, and Sanskrit. Innovation robotics maker-space and vocational skill tasters as per NEP 2020.

Phase 3 Culmination Class 9 to Class 12

CBSE Secondary & Senior Secondary Excellence (K-12)

Full CBSE Board Examination affiliation offering Science (Medical/Non-Medical), Commerce, and Humanities streams, supported by competitive test preparation and career guidance.
