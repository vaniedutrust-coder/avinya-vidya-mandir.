Alumni Mentorship Initiatives

Our graduates actively invest their time and wisdom into supporting current Avinya students through career guidance and masterclasses.

☕

Coffee with a Senior

One-on-one virtual mentoring sessions connecting middle school students with university seniors for advice on course selection, study habits, and entrance prep.

💻

Alumni STEM Masterclasses

Interactive weekend workshops where alumni from IITs, DTU, and tech companies return to campus to conduct live AI coding, electronics, and design thinking labs.

👥

House Leadership Dialogues

Former House Captains address Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, and Amber houses during morning assemblies, inspiring discipline, team spirit, and sportsmanship.
