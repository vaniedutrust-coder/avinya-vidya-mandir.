Purpose-Built Infrastructure

Campus Facilities Grouped by Educational Purpose

Every space at Avinya is deliberately planned to give young learners room to move, discover, create, and feel at home.

📚

1. Early Learning & Discovery Studios

Active • Foundational
Montessori & Phonics

Daylight-Filled Activity Studios

Designed with rounded child-safe wooden furniture, low open shelving, tactile sandpaper sound cards, reading nooks, and ample natural sunlight that supports healthy visual development.

✨ Highlights: Non-toxic materials, sensory book corners & ergonomic seating
Tactile & STEM

Junior STEM & Tactile Discovery Hub

Equipped with Montessori counting rods, kinetic gears, geometric solid blocks, balance scales, and sensory sorting stations where children learn foundational concepts through hands-on play.

🔬 Highlights: Concrete math manipulatives & early engineering kits
🏃

2. Sports Turf & Nature Courtyards

Active • Outdoor
Athletics & Fitness

Green Sports Turf & Athletic Arena

Cushioned synthetic multi-sport turf with soft-impact running lanes, mini hurdle circuits, balance courses, and open play space designed specifically for safe early-childhood gross-motor development.

🏃 Highlights: Impact-absorbing turf, sprint lanes & obstacle challenges
Botanical Zone

Open-Air Nature Courtyard & Botanicals

Sunlit assembly courtyard framed by flowering planters and herbal garden beds where children observe plant growth, practice morning breathing, and celebrate eco-friendly cultural festivals.

🌱 Highlights: Fresh air assembly space, potted gardens & Earth Day flora
🎭

3. Performing Arts & Community Arenas

Active • Campus Life
Performing Arts

Cultural Stage & Assembly Amphitheater

Open-air stage with professional acoustic setup and proscenium lighting for theatrical plays, classical dance performances, shloka recitations, and joyous annual celebrations.

🎭 Highlights: Stage acoustics, proscenium lighting & festival seating
Parent Helpdesk

Parent Interaction & Admissions Lounge

Comfortable, air-conditioned reception and consultation lounges where parents can discuss educational pathways with our counselors, view academic portfolios, and receive fee guidance.

🤝 Highlights: Private counseling desks & comfortable waiting suites
Verified Security & Care

Child Safety & Institutional Safeguards

From verified security personnel and continuous CCTV monitoring to child-height handrails and age-appropriate sanitary facilities, every square foot is optimized for early childhood protection.

📹

100% CCTV Perimeter Coverage

High-definition cameras monitor all common corridors, playgrounds, entry gates, and activity arenas throughout the school day.

🚸

Photo Escort Card Identification

Students are handed over exclusively to parents or authorized guardians carrying the official school-issued photo escort card.

🩺

On-Campus First Aid & Medical Care

Equipped campus first-aid station with pediatric emergency tie-ups, certified medical supplies, and immediate parental notification.

🚌

GPS-Tracked School Transport

School buses with GPS tracking, speed governors, and trained female attendants onboard covering Burari and surrounding North Delhi routes.

💧

Multi-Stage RO Purified Water

Multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration systems with periodic water quality testing to guarantee 100% pure drinking water.

🚻

Toddler-Height Sanitized Restrooms

Child-height sanitation fixtures, non-slip flooring, and dedicated female support nannies providing gentle early-years assistance.
