Space to Breathe, Learn & Play

A 1-Acre Campus Built for Growing Minds

Surrounded by fresh air and sunlight in Burari, Delhi, our 1-acre campus provides the perfect balance of green natural outdoor space and modern child-centric indoor environments.

Athletics

Green Sports Turf & Athletic Arena

Equipped with synthetic running lanes, hurdle circuits, soccer nets, and soft-cushioned ground for safe, injury-free daily play.

Learning Studios

Daylight-Filled Activity Studios

Ergonomic rounded furniture, Montessori wooden apparatus, phonics walls, reading carpets, and digital interactive learning panels.

Security

Secure Gated Entry & Surveillance

Single-point security access, full CCTV perimeter coverage, visitor logbooks, and escort card authentication for all child pickups.

Botanical Zone

Open-Air Nature Courtyard

Potted botanical displays, herbal garden beds, and sunlight-drenched courtyards for morning assemblies and Earth Day activities.

Parent Helpdesk

Modern Parent Interaction Lounge

Air-conditioned counselor lounges, admission helpdesk, fee counseling desk, and comfortable family waiting zones.

Performing Arts

Multi-Purpose Stage & Arena

Stage with proscenium lighting and acoustics for theatrical plays, dance performances, annual days, and community festivals.

Parent Peace of Mind

Zero-Compromise Safety Standards

From verified security personnel to age-appropriate ergonomic sanitary facilities, every square foot is optimized for early childhood protection.

📹

360° CCTV Monitoring

High-definition cameras monitor all common corridors, playgrounds, entry gates, and activity arenas around the clock.

🚸

Escort Card Identification

Students are released exclusively to parents or authorized guardians carrying school-issued photo escort cards.

🩺

Infirmary & First Aid Care

Dedicated campus first-aid station with trained nursing staff, pediatric emergency tie-ups, and emergency ambulance access.

🚌

GPS-Tracked Safe Transport

School buses with GPS tracking, speed governors, and trained female attendants onboard on all routes across Burari and North Delhi.

💧

RO Purified Water Stations

Multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) filtration systems with regular certified water testing to ensure 100% hygienic drinking water.

🚻

Child-Scale Sanitized Washrooms

Toddler-height sanitized toilets, non-slip flooring, and dedicated female nannies for early years toilet assistance.

Quick Route Guides from North Delhi Hubs

Clear driving directions connecting surrounding residential and academic corridors to our 1-acre campus:

From Burari Chowk / Main Market ~4–5 Mins (1.8 km)

Drive straight toward Shankarpura via Virander Nagar Road near Aqua Garden. Our green gates and 1-acre campus perimeter are easily accessible with ample visitor parking.

🏁 Landmark: Near Aqua Garden • Virander Nagar
From Jahangirpuri Metro (Yellow Line) ~10–12 Mins (4.5 km)

Exit via GT Karnal Road / Outer Ring Road connector straight into Burari. Rapid access with regular feeder e-rickshaws and dedicated Avinya school transport fleet.

🚌 Metro Connect: Dedicated school bus feeder routes
From Model Town & Derawal Nagar ~14–16 Mins (6.8 km)

Head via Kingsway Camp or Ring Road flyover towards Burari boulevard. Smooth, signal-free drive connecting Model Town directly to our green, open campus.

🚗 Arterial Highway: Wide signal-free approach road
From Timarpur, Mukherjee Nagar & DU ~12–14 Mins (5.6 km)

Take the Outer Ring Road corridor along Nehru Vihar / Wazirabad junction into Burari main road. Seamless commute for Delhi University and North Campus faculty families.

🎓 DU Corridor: Popular with North Campus faculty
