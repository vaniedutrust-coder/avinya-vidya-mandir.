🛠️ TRANSPARENT HIRING

Our 4-Step Recruitment Process

We value your time as much as your expertise. Our hiring process is clear, transparent, and structured to understand your unique educational strengths.

1

Application & Screening

Submit your CV, qualifications, and teaching philosophy below. Our Academic Panel reviews applications within 48 hours.

2

Telephonic Dialogue

A 20-minute exploratory conversation with our HR team to discuss your background, pedagogical style, and career expectations.

3

Micro-Teaching Demo

A 25-minute live classroom teaching demonstration on a topic of your choice, followed by an interview with the Principal & Academic Head.

4

Formal Offer & Induction

Transparent discussion of remuneration, issue of appointment letter, background verification, and a guided 2-week buddy onboarding.
