Quick Route Guides from North Delhi Hubs

Direct driving access from surrounding residential and academic corridors to our 1-acre campus:

From Burari Chowk / Main Market ~4–5 Mins (1.8 km)

Drive straight towards Shankarpura via Virander Nagar Road near Aqua Garden. Our green gates and 1-acre campus perimeter are easily accessible with ample visitor parking.

🏁 Landmark: Near Aqua Garden • Virander Nagar
From Jahangirpuri Metro (Yellow Line) ~10–12 Mins (4.5 km)

Exit via GT Karnal Road / Outer Ring Road connector straight into Burari. Rapid access with regular feeder e-rickshaws and dedicated Avinya school transport fleet.

🚌 Metro Connect: Dedicated school bus feeder routes
From Model Town & Derawal Nagar ~14–16 Mins (6.8 km)

Head via Kingsway Camp or Ring Road flyover towards Burari boulevard. Smooth, signal-free drive connecting Model Town directly to our green, open campus.

🚗 Arterial Highway: Wide signal-free approach road
From Timarpur, Mukherjee Nagar & DU ~12–14 Mins (5.6 km)

Take the Outer Ring Road corridor along Nehru Vihar / Wazirabad junction into Burari main road. Seamless commute for Delhi University and North Campus faculty families.

🎓 DU Corridor: Popular with North Campus faculty
