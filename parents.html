👔 DRESS CODE POLICY

Pride in Identity: Uniform & Grooming Standards

Our neat, breathable cotton-rich uniforms foster equality, mutual respect, and active comfort for every learner.

Everyday Wear

Summer Regular Uniform

Worn on Monday, Tuesday, Thursday & Friday
  • Boys (Foundational & Primary): Half-sleeve sky-blue polo shirt with school crest, navy tailored shorts/trousers, navy socks with golden tipping, black formal leather shoes.
  • Girls (Foundational & Primary): Sky-blue polo shirt with school crest, pleated navy skirt or comfortable trousers, navy knee-high socks, black formal buckle shoes, navy hair accessories.
  • Middle School (Boys & Girls): Formal sky-blue collared shirt with school tie, tailored navy trousers, formal black leather shoes, black leather belt.
Sports & Athletics

Wednesday House Uniform

Worn every Wednesday for Sports & House Meets
  • House Dry-Fit Sports T-Shirt: Breathable moisture-wicking active shirt in assigned House color (Ruby Red, Sapphire Blue, Emerald Green, Amber Gold) with mascot crest.
  • Athletic Performance Track Pants: Navy blue comfortable stretch track pants with lateral house-colored side piping.
  • Footwear: Clean all-white athletic running sneakers with non-marking rubber soles and white sports socks.
Winter Season

Winter Formal Uniform

Mandatory from November 15 through February 28
  • Full-Sleeve Collared Shirt: Crisp formal sky-blue shirt paired with school navy necktie and winter trousers.
  • School Crest Blazer & Pullover: Single-breasted deep navy woollen blazer with embossed brass school crest buttons; V-neck sleeveless navy woollen sweater.
  • Accessories: Navy woollen cap/beanie, warm navy gloves, and thick woollen socks/stockings for cold mornings.

To protect young spines, students only carry a light bag with lunch and daily homework diaries. All heavy textbooks, art materials, and reference books remain safely stored in individual student classroom cubbies and subject laboratory lockers.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for Avinya Parents

Quick clarity on daily school operations, parent communication channels, and logistics.

Simply click on the “Forgot Password / Instant OTP” tab on the Parent Portal login screen. Enter your 10-digit registered mobile number to receive a 6-digit one-time password via SMS. If you have recently changed your mobile number, please contact our administrative desk at +91 9911102005 or email parents@avinyaschool.com to update your records.

To maintain uninterrupted instructional focus in our classrooms, unexpected walk-ins during school hours are not permitted. However, every teacher has designated weekly parent consultation slots between 2:15 PM and 3:00 PM. You can schedule a meeting effortlessly through the Parent ERP app or by calling the school reception 24 hours in advance.

For planned early departures, please submit an early-leave request via the Parent ERP app before 8:30 AM. An authorized parent or guardian carrying the official Parent ID Card must collect the student from the administrative reception. No student is handed over to unverified individuals without prior written authorization.

Birthdays are celebrated joyfully! Students are permitted to wear clean civil clothes on their special day. In line with our healthy nutrition and equality philosophy, students may only distribute small, sealed toffees or donate a storybook to the class library. Expensive gifts, cakes, and return gifts are strictly discouraged.

Every school bus is equipped with a dual AIS-140 GPS tracker and CCTV cameras. Once your child is enrolled in the transport system, the ‘Bus Tracker’ tile on your Avinya Connect app becomes live. You receive an alert when the bus starts its trip and an automated SMS notification when the bus is 2 stops away from your pickup point.

School fees are due quarterly (April, July, October, January). You can pay via UPI, NetBanking, or Debit/Credit card directly in the ERP app without transaction charges. Automated fee receipts and annual tuition fee certificates for Section 80C tax rebates are generated immediately in the ‘Fee Receipts’ repository in the portal.
