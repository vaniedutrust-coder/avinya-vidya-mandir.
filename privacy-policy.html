Privacy Policy

Last updated: Academic Session 2026–2027

Avinya Vidya Mandir respects the privacy of our parents, students, and website visitors. We collect personal information (such as student names, dates of birth, parent contact numbers, and emails) solely to facilitate admissions inquiries, process campus tour bookings, and communicate academic updates.
