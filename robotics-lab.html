🔬 HANDS-ON EXPERIENTIAL LEARNING

Four Pillars of the Avinya STEAM Laboratory

Allying with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) vision of experiential discovery, our lab bridges play-way curiosity with structural engineering mindsets.

🧩

Early Computational Thinking

Screen-free coding puzzles, pattern sequences, and directional Bee-Bot floor robots that instill algorithmic sequencing in early foundational learners.

⚙️

Tactile Mechanics & LEGO Kits

Modular building sets featuring pulleys, levers, gear ratios, and snap-circuits where students assemble motorized models and test mechanical laws.

🌱

1-Acre Eco-Science Zone

Bridging the lab with nature: soil testing stations, seed germination observation chambers, and weather monitoring gauges across our green campus.

💡

Junior Tinkering & Maker Space

Safe maker workbenches with tactile materials, clay prototyping, optical prisms, and magnetic discovery stations that make science fun and tangible.

📈 GRADE-BY-GRADE PROGRESSION

The Avinya STEAM Curriculum Pathway

How our robotics and innovation program evolves as our students advance grade-by-grade annually towards full CBSE K-12 standards.

Ages 2–5 • Foundational

Playgroup, Nursery & Kindergarten

  • Sensory coding mats & color-coded tactile cards
  • Symmetry and structural building blocks
  • Nature identification & leaf classification on campus
  • Story-based sequence mapping & phonics logic
Age 6+ • Primary Foundation

Class 1 (Active Academic Grade)

  • Scratch Jr. drag-and-drop animation & storytelling
  • Motorized LEGO machines & basic gear linkages
  • Simple electrical circuits (LEDs, buzzers, switches)
  • Mathematics lab manipulative exercises & geometry
Expanding Annually • K-12 Horizon

Middle & Senior School Vision

  • Microcontroller programming (Arduino, BBC micro:bit)
  • Python coding & introductory Artificial Intelligence
  • CBSE Science Conclaves, Atal Tinkering Olympiads
  • Composite physics, chemistry & biology labs
